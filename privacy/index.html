Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Android apps I develop and to the content published on this site.

Data Collection

I only collect the minimum data necessary for app functionality. Where an app requires personal data (e.g., sign-in, sync), it will be stated clearly in the app description and a consent prompt will be shown.

Usage & Analytics

Apps may use anonymous analytics to understand usage patterns and improve performance. Collected analytics data is aggregated and does not identify individuals.

Third-Party Services

Some apps may integrate third-party services (e.g., crash reporting, in-app libraries). These services have their own privacy policies — check the specific app's Play Store listing for details.

Data Retention & Requests

If you request deletion of any personal data associated with an app, contact me at thezerodeveloper@example.com and I will respond promptly.

Children

My apps are not intended for children under 13. If an app is targeted at younger users, appropriate measures and parental consent will be implemented.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact me at thezerodeveloper@gmail.com.
